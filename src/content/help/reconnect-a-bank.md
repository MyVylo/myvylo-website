Banks sometimes need fresh authorization or another verification step. Use **Reconnect** on the existing institution so you keep working with that connection.

The provider will guide you through any bank sign-in, code, or approval it needs. When you return to Vylo, let the import finish before checking recent purchases.

## Why does verification keep repeating?

Sign in to your bank’s own app and check for notices or required account updates. If the reconnect flow still loops or fails, email **hi@myvylo.com** with the bank name and error message.

Adding the same bank again can create duplicate accounts. Reconnect is the better starting point for an existing connection that needs attention.
