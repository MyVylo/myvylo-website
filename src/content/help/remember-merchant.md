**Remember merchant** saves your category choice for future matching purchases. A regular internet bill is a good example: choose Internet once and let Vylo reuse it.

On the iPhone review card, the same choice is called **Always use this category**.

## What if this purchase is an exception?

Leave the option off. That changes the current transaction without saving a new merchant rule. This is useful at a store where you buy different things on different visits.

## Will old purchases change too?

Don’t assume a saved merchant choice rewrites all earlier transactions. Check older purchases separately if you want to correct their categories.

If future purchases keep going to the wrong place, check that merchant’s saved choice and the category’s [custom keywords](/help/articles/category-rules/).
