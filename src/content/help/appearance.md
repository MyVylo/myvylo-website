Change the theme in **Settings → Appearance**. This changes how Vylo looks, not your household’s data.

## What does the eye button hide?

On iPhone, the eye button on supported main screens hides financial values on your display. Tap it again and complete Face ID or passcode verification to show them.

It doesn’t hide accounts from another household member or change their permissions. [Read about household sharing](/help/articles/household-visibility/).
