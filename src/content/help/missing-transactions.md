A purchase may be in another month, hidden by a filter, or still waiting for the bank to send it.

## Has it posted at the bank?

If it’s pending, give it time to finish processing. Some banks only provide the posted version. Check **Accounts** for a reconnect warning or a delayed sync.

If a posted purchase is still missing after another day, email **hi@myvylo.com** with the bank name, merchant, approximate date, and last-sync status. One example is enough to start.

Avoid adding a manual copy while an import is still running; it may count twice when the bank sends the purchase.
