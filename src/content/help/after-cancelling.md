When you cancel a paid subscription, you keep access for the time you’ve already paid for. **Settings → Billing** shows when that access ends. For App Store purchases, Apple’s subscription page shows the expiration date.

## Does cancelling delete my data?

No. Cancellation stops renewal; [account deletion](/help/articles/delete-account/) is a separate action. Download any records you want to keep before paid access ends.

## Can I come back?

Yes. Billing shows the option to resume or restart when it’s available for your subscription. Confirm the price and renewal date before continuing.

If you already have an active Apple subscription, [restore that purchase](/help/articles/restore-subscription/) instead of buying a second plan.
