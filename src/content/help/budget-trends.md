Budget trends help you spot categories that are rising, falling, or regularly exceeding the plan. Open a category’s transactions when you want to understand a surprising month.

## What makes a fair comparison?

A partial month won’t tell the same story as a completed one. Missing bank history, an annual bill, or a category you recently reorganized can also explain a change.

One expensive month doesn’t erase your progress. Look for a pattern before changing the plan.

[Monthly reports](/help/articles/monthly-reports/) provide another way to review a completed month.
