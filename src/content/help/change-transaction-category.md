Changing a category changes where Vylo counts the transaction in your spending and budget. It doesn’t change the amount or payment at your bank.

If the category you need is missing, [create it first](/help/articles/add-a-category/). If the transaction is pending and editing is unavailable, wait for the posted version.

To make the same choice for future purchases, use [Remember merchant](/help/articles/remember-merchant/). Leave it off for a one-time exception.
