Moving a category keeps it and its transactions, but places it under a different group. Deleting a category removes it and asks what to do with its transactions.

## How do I combine two categories?

Keep one category. Delete the other and choose to move its transactions to the category you’re keeping. Then check the remaining category’s budget and rules.

For expense categories, you can also move transactions to Uncategorized and review them later. Income has separate reassignment rules, and Salary can be renamed but not deleted.

If you only want a different label, [rename the category](/help/articles/rename-a-category/).
