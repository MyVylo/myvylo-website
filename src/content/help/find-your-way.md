The web and iPhone apps show the same household. Their navigation is arranged to suit each screen.

## Where should I start?

| What you want to see | On the web | On iPhone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This month at a glance | Dashboard | Home |
| Purchases and payments | Transactions | Activity |
| Your spending plan | Budgets | Budget |
| Assets and debts | Net Worth | Net Worth |
| Category names and rules | Categories | Settings → Categories |
| Connected banks | Accounts | Settings → Accounts |
| Household members | Household | Settings → Household Members |
| Monthly reports | Reports | Settings → Reports |
| Billing and privacy | Settings | Settings |

Check the month selector if you’re looking for a purchase or budget from a different month.
