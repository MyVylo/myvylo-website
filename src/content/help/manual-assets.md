A manual asset or debt lets you include something in Net Worth that Vylo can’t update through a bank. Choose the correct type and enter its value or amount owed.

## How do I keep it current?

On iPhone, open the item and edit its value when it changes. Manual values don’t update from a property website, lender, or valuation service.

A home and its mortgage are separate items. If the mortgage is already connected, don’t add a second manual copy of that debt.
