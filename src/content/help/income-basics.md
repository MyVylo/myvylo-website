Income transactions can arrive from a connected bank, or you can enter them manually. Use the appropriate income category, such as Salary, for money you earned.

## Where can I see my income?

Open the **Income** card on your overview: **Dashboard** on the web, or **Home** on iPhone. **Income activity** lists the payments for the selected month, including their dates, amounts, and income categories.

Your custom income categories appear on the individual payments. They don’t each get a separate summary card. On iPhone, select **Total** to see all income types in the activity list.

If a payment is missing, check the selected month and find it in Transactions on the web or Activity on iPhone. A deposit categorized as a transfer or refund won’t be treated as earnings.

## Can I keep side-gig income separate from salary?

Yes. [Create a category](/help/articles/add-a-category/) in the **Income** group, such as Tutoring or Freelance design, then assign those payments to it. You’ll see that name beside each payment in Income activity.

Use a separate expense category for the costs of doing that work. [This example covers both income and expenses](/help/articles/side-gig-income-and-expenses/).

## Is every deposit income?

No. A transfer from your savings account or a refund on a purchase isn’t new salary. Categorize it for what it represents. [Read about transfers and refunds](/help/articles/transfers-and-refunds/).

## Expected income and actual income

Expected income helps you plan a budget. Actual income records what has arrived. If your pay varies, check the assumption in your budget each month.

Add income manually only when it won’t come through a connected bank. Otherwise, you may count the same payment twice.
