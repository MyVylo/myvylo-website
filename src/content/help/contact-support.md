Email **[hi@myvylo.com](mailto:hi@myvylo.com)** for help with Vylo. Questions before signing up are welcome too.

Tell us what you were trying to do and whether you’re using the web or iPhone app. If there’s an error, include its wording. For a bank issue, the bank name and last-sync status help.

A screenshot can be useful, but hide unrelated purchases and account numbers. We’ll never ask you to send passwords or verification codes.

## Have an idea?

We love hearing what would make Vylo better. We read customer suggestions and move quickly on useful improvements. A few sentences about what’s frustrating you are plenty.
