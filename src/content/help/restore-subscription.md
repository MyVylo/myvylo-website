First check that you’re signed into the Vylo account and household that should have access. A different email address or an unaccepted household invitation can make a subscription appear missing.

## Restoring an Apple purchase

Use **Restore Purchases** in Vylo while signed into the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restoring checks the existing purchase; it doesn’t mean you need to buy another subscription.

If Apple shows an active Vylo subscription but access still isn’t restored, email us with the receipt reference and your Vylo account email.

## Restoring a web subscription

Check **Settings → Billing**. An active plan should already provide access. An ending or inactive plan may offer a resume or restart option; confirm the price and renewal terms before continuing.
