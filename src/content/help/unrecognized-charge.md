A bank description may show a payment processor or a company’s registered name instead of the shop you recognize. Compare the date and amount with receipts and order confirmations.

In a shared household, ask whether another member recognizes it. It may be a subscription renewal or a purchase that posted a few days after you made it.

## What if it wasn’t authorized?

Contact your bank or card issuer through its official app or the number on your card. It can help with a dispute or card restriction. Vylo cannot reverse the charge or freeze a card.

You can skip the transaction in Vylo’s review queue while you investigate it.

## Vylo shows something different from the bank

Email **hi@myvylo.com** with the bank name, date, and the difference you see. Hide account numbers and unrelated purchases in any screenshot.
