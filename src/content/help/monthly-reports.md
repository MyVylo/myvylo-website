A monthly report summarizes spending, leading categories, and changes during a completed month. When a budget exists, it can compare spending with that plan too.

A report-ready notification is a shortcut. You can return to **Reports** later even if you dismissed it.

## Why isn’t a month available?

A new household may not have an eligible report yet, and the current month isn’t complete. Reports also depend on the transaction history available to Vylo and completed processing.

If an expected past month is still missing after imports finish, email **hi@myvylo.com** with the month.

## Can I check the purchases behind a number?

Yes. Use the report’s category and transaction details to see what contributed to it. Reviewing uncategorized purchases makes the breakdown more useful.
