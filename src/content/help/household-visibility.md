Everyone in a Vylo household uses their own login and sees the household’s financial information. That includes connected accounts, transactions, notes, categories, budgets, and manually entered financial details.

**A separate login does not make your connected accounts private from the household.** Only invite someone you want to share that information with.

## Who manages the household?

The owner manages members and household data exports. Subscription changes belong to the billing owner; App Store subscriptions must be managed through the Apple Account that purchased them.

A member may see the subscription status without controls to cancel or change the payment method.

## Who can allow AI categorization?

Each person decides for the bank accounts they connected. Another household member cannot consent on their behalf. [Read how AI consent works](/help/articles/ai-categorization/).

## Why do we see different accounts or budgets?

You may have created separate households. Check that both people joined using the same household invitation. [Get help with an invitation](/help/articles/invitation-problems/).
