Search for the bank’s full name and check that you’ve chosen the right country and service. Personal banking, business banking, and credit cards may have separate listings.

## My bank is listed, but an account is missing

The provider may not support that account type, even if it supports other accounts at the bank. A temporary bank outage can also prevent a connection from completing.

Email **hi@myvylo.com** with the bank name, country, account type, and any error message. We’ll check the connection options.

## Can I keep using Vylo?

Yes. You can [enter transactions manually](/help/articles/add-a-transaction/) and [add manual assets or debts](/help/articles/manual-assets/) while an account is unsupported. Those entries won’t update from the bank automatically.
