Manual transactions are useful for a cash purchase, a payment to a babysitter, or income from an account you haven’t connected.

The date you enter determines which month includes the transaction. Enter the merchant, amount, date, and category.

## Will this duplicate a bank purchase?

It can. If you used a connected card, the purchase may still arrive automatically. Check [bank-sync timing](/help/articles/how-often-transactions-sync/) before entering it by hand.
