A grocery purchase on your credit card is spending. Paying that credit card from your chequing account is moving money you already spent—it shouldn’t count as another grocery purchase.

## Transfers between your accounts

Use **Transfers** or **CC Payments** under the **Excluded** group in the transaction’s category picker. Vylo has rules to recognize common money movements, but you can correct one that was mistaken for an expense.

A transfer to another person may still be spending. Paying a babysitter by e-transfer belongs in your childcare category, for example.

## Refunds and reimbursements

Check the original purchase and the money coming back. Use **Refunds** under **Excluded** for a returned purchase. Don’t count it as salary.

Incoming refunds and reimbursements don’t automatically subtract from the original category’s budget spending. If you want a fully reimbursed work expense outside the household budget, you can put both the purchase and repayment in an excluded category.

[See the reimbursement example and how excluded categories work](/help/articles/excluded-categories/).
