Your bank may show an **available balance** that includes pending holds and a **current balance** based on posted activity. Make sure you’re comparing the same kind of balance for the same account.

## Why did my transactions update but not my balance?

The two feeds can arrive separately. A recent payment may therefore appear in the transaction list before the balance reflects it, or the other way around.

## What else should I check?

Look for a duplicate account or a manual asset that repeats a connected balance. If the difference remains after syncing, email us the bank name, masked account label, and the two balances you’re comparing.

Changing transaction categories won’t fix a delayed bank balance.
