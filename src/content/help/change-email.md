Your current email remains active until the new one is verified. Vylo asks for your current password before sending a verification code to the new address.

Once the change is confirmed, use the new address to sign in.

## How do I change my password?

In **Settings → Privacy & Security**, use the password-reset link control and follow the email. If you can’t sign in, use the reset option on the sign-in screen.

Changing your password revokes existing trusted sign-ins and refresh sessions. [Review signed-in devices](/help/articles/signed-in-devices/) too if you suspect someone accessed the account.
