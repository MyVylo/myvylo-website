You can cancel renewal without emailing us. **Settings → Billing** tells you who manages the subscription.

For a paid subscription, cancellation keeps access until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Check the ending date after confirming.

## If Apple bills you

Cancel through the Apple Account that purchased Vylo. On iPhone, open **Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Vylo → Cancel Subscription**. Apple shows an expiration message when a subscription is already cancelled. [Apple’s cancellation instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).

Deleting the app or your Vylo account does **not** cancel Apple billing. For an App Store subscription, cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

## Why is there no cancel button in Vylo?

You may be a household member rather than the billing owner, Apple may manage the subscription, or renewal may already be off. Check the status in Billing.

If you still need help, email **hi@myvylo.com** with your account email and whether you pay through Vylo or Apple.
