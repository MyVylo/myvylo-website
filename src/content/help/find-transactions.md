Choose the month you want, then search for the merchant. If the shop’s usual name finds nothing, try part of the description shown by your bank.

## Still can’t find it?

Clear any filters and check the month when the purchase posted. A late-month purchase may post in the following month.

If it still isn’t there, check [missing transactions](/help/articles/missing-transactions/). A pending purchase or delayed bank sync may explain it.
