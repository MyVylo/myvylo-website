First check that the budget and transaction list show the same month. A purchase made at month-end may post in the next month.

## What usually causes the difference?

- A purchase is in the wrong category.
- A recent transaction hasn’t synced yet.
- A manual purchase was also imported from the bank.
- The same bank account is connected twice.
- A transfer or credit-card payment was counted as spending.

Open the category’s transactions to find the entry behind the total. [Change its category](/help/articles/change-transaction-category/) if needed, then let the budget refresh.

## Is “Left to spend” my bank balance?

No. It’s your planned spending minus the spending counted so far. Your bank balance also includes savings, payment timing, and other account movements.

If a total still looks wrong, email us the month, category, and the two amounts you’re comparing.
