**Settings → Privacy & Security** lists active sessions and marks the current device. Other entries show their browser or device label and recent activity.

A browser label may be broad, so check the timing as well as the name. Sign out unfamiliar sessions and reset your password if you suspect unauthorized access.

## Is this the same as clearing trusted devices?

No. Signing out ends an active session. Clearing trusted devices removes remembered verification, so later sign-ins need a code again.

If you need help with an unfamiliar session, email **hi@myvylo.com**.
