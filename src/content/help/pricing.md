**$2 for your first month, then $7/month after that. No annual commitments. Cancel anytime.**

Your household members are included. They join your household with their own login and don’t need a separate subscription.

## Where can I see my next payment?

Open **Settings → Billing**. You’ll find your subscription status and its next payment date—or the date access ends if you’ve cancelled.

## What about tax and currency?

The checkout shows the currency and any applicable tax before you pay. If you subscribe through the iPhone app, Apple shows the price and terms for that purchase. An introductory price on the web may differ from an App Store offer.

## Can I cancel after the first month?

Yes. You can [cancel renewal](/help/articles/cancel-subscription/) at any time. A paid subscription stays available until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
