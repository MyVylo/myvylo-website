Net Worth shows changes in the included account balances and manual values. Choose a period to see the available history.

## When do trends become available?

Vylo needs seven consecutive days of net worth observations before trends unlock. Connecting a bank starts collecting those balances; it does not recreate a complete balance history from past purchases.

## Why does the chart start recently?

Importing 90 days of transactions doesn’t guarantee 90 days of historical balances. A new connection or manually added asset may have much less balance history.

An empty earlier period isn’t a zero balance.

## What explains a sudden jump?

A new account, an updated home value, a removed debt, or a duplicate item can change the total. Check the assets and liabilities before treating the change as income or investment performance.
