The billing owner can manage a web subscription’s card and invoices in **Settings → Billing**. Other household members may see the status without edit controls.

## Why are there no invoices?

Check whether a payment has completed and whether this is a web subscription. Apple provides receipts for App Store purchases; those payments won’t appear in Vylo’s web invoice list.

## What if I just want to cancel?

Use [Cancel your subscription](/help/articles/cancel-subscription/). Removing a card and updating a card are different actions; the confirmation explains any effect on renewal.

Payment details belong in the secure payment form. Don’t email a card number or security code.
