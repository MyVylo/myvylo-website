Email **[hi@myvylo.com](mailto:hi@myvylo.com?subject=An%20idea%20for%20Vylo)**. We love hearing ideas from customers, and useful suggestions often turn into improvements quickly.

Tell us what you want to do and where Vylo gets in the way. For example: “I want to separate school expenses from childcare without rebuilding my budget.” You don’t need to design a solution first.

## What happens next?

We read every suggestion and may ask a question to understand it better. Small changes can ship quickly; bigger ones take more work. We’ll be straight with you about what’s possible.

See [What’s new](/updates/) for recent changes and what’s coming next.
