**Auto-build from spending** uses your transaction history to suggest a monthly budget. It gives you a starting point; you choose the amounts to keep.

The preview uses available spending from the past 90 days. An unusual purchase or incomplete import can affect the averages, so check the suggestions before applying them.

## Why is budget setup waiting for data?

Your first bank import needs to finish before Vylo can calculate spending-based amounts. Leave the connection in place while it finishes.

You can also [start from scratch](/help/articles/budget-from-scratch/) or [change amounts later](/help/articles/edit-a-budget/). A budget sets spending targets in Vylo; it doesn’t move money between bank accounts.
