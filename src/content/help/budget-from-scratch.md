Choose **Start from scratch** when you already know your planned amounts, or when past spending isn’t a useful guide to the next month.

Include regular bills and everyday spending. Check the total against the income you expect before applying the budget.

If a category is missing, [add it in Categories](/help/articles/add-a-category/) and return to budget setup. You can [edit the plan later](/help/articles/edit-a-budget/) as your needs change.
