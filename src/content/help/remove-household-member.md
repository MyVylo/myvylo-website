Removing a member ends their access to the shared household. Cancelling a pending invitation prevents that invitation from being used; it doesn’t remove someone who has already joined.

If the person connected bank accounts or manages the subscription, contact **hi@myvylo.com** before removing them so we can help you account for those connections and payments.

## Can I leave or transfer ownership?

Email us with what you want to keep and who should manage the household. Deleting the owner’s account deletes the household; it does not transfer it to another member.
