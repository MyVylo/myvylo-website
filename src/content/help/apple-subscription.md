If Vylo’s Billing screen says the subscription is managed through the App Store, use the Apple Account that made the purchase to manage it.

You can open **Manage Apple Subscription** from Vylo when shown, or go to **iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Vylo**. Apple shows the renewal or expiration date there.

## Why can’t I find the subscription?

Check the Apple Account on the purchase receipt. You may be signed into a different one. A subscription bought on Vylo’s website won’t appear in Apple’s subscription list.

## Do I need to subscribe again to use the iPhone app?

No. Sign in to the Vylo account and household that already has access. Buying another plan won’t connect two separate households.

For renewal, follow [the cancellation guide](/help/articles/cancel-subscription/). For a charge made by Apple, [request a refund through Apple](/help/articles/billing-refund/).
