**Export CSV** downloads transactions in a file you can open in Excel, Numbers, or another spreadsheet app. Choose the month and filters first, then check the file’s dates if you need a particular period.

For a broader copy of your account and household records, use [Download your data](/help/articles/download-your-data/).
