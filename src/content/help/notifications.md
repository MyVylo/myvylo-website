Vylo’s notification settings let you choose which updates you receive and how they arrive. Options include in-app notices, push notifications, and email where available.

Budget-alert emails are grouped into a daily roundup. Turning off a report notification doesn’t delete the report; it stays available in Reports.

## Why didn’t a notification arrive?

Check the preference in Vylo, then the browser or iPhone’s notification permission. For email, check spam and the address in your profile.

Security and essential account messages are separate from optional spending updates.
