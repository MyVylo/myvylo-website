A **category** tells you what a transaction was for: Groceries, Dining out, or Salary. A **group** holds related categories, such as Groceries and Dining out under Flexible.

Your household shares one category list. You can [rename a category](/help/articles/rename-a-category/), [add a new one](/help/articles/add-a-category/), or [organize groups](/help/articles/category-groups/).

For the full starting list, see [Vylo’s default categories, with definitions and examples](/help/articles/default-categories/).

## Should I rename a category or create another one?

Rename it when the same spending needs a clearer label. Create a new category when you want to track that spending separately—for example, Childcare and School expenses instead of a single Kids category.

Renaming keeps the transactions already assigned to the category. Creating another category gives you a separate category with no history assigned to it yet.

## Does changing a name change the sorting?

No. A new name alone doesn’t create new merchant matches. Use [Remember merchant](/help/articles/remember-merchant/) or [custom keywords](/help/articles/category-rules/) to change where future purchases go.
