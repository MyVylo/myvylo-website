The invitation should go to the email address the person uses for Vylo. If the address is wrong, the owner can cancel the pending invitation and send another.

## The email hasn’t arrived

Check spam and search the inbox for Vylo. The household owner can resend a pending invitation from the member list. [See the invitation steps](/help/articles/invite-household/).

## The link expired

Ask the owner to resend it, then open the newest email. An older link doesn’t become valid again when a new invitation is sent.

## We already created separate households

Email **hi@myvylo.com** with both account email addresses and tell us which household you want to share. We’ll check the existing accounts and subscriptions before you make changes.

Don’t delete a household to fix an invitation problem; it may already contain financial history or an active subscription.
