Yes. You can enter expenses and income yourself, and add assets or debts that aren’t connected to a bank.

Use [manual transactions](/help/articles/add-a-transaction/) for cash purchases or income Vylo won’t receive automatically. Use [manual assets and debts](/help/articles/manual-assets/) for things such as a home, vehicle, or unsupported loan.

## What needs updating by hand?

Manual transactions stay as you entered them. Manual account values also need you to update them; Vylo can’t fetch a new value from an unconnected bank or lender.

## What if I connect the bank later?

Check for overlap with entries you already made. A manually entered card purchase and its later bank import can count as two transactions.

If your bank is supported but a purchase is simply late, [check the sync status](/help/articles/missing-transactions/) before entering another copy.
