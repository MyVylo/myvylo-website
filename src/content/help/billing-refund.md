For a payment made on Vylo’s website, email **[hi@myvylo.com](mailto:hi@myvylo.com)** from your account email. Include the charge date, amount, and invoice reference if you have one. Tell us what looks wrong and we’ll check it.

## What about App Store charges?

Apple decides refunds for its purchases. Sign in to [Apple’s refund service](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/), choose the refund-request option, and select the charge. [Apple explains the process here](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).

A refund request doesn’t necessarily stop future renewal. [Cancel the subscription](/help/articles/cancel-subscription/) too if you don’t want another billing period.
