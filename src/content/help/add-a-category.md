Add a category when you want to track something separately—for example, Books or Pet care. If an existing category just needs a clearer name, [rename it](/help/articles/rename-a-category/) instead.

After creating it, assign a transaction to the new category. Use [Remember merchant](/help/articles/remember-merchant/) when future purchases from that merchant should go there too.

## Can I add my own income categories?

Yes. Add the category inside the **Income** group. You could use Freelance design, Tutoring, or Weekend photography to keep those payments separate from your salary.

Then assign the incoming payments to that income category. Creating a category doesn’t move existing transactions into it automatically. The payments appear in **Income activity** with your category name. [See where to find your income](/help/articles/income-basics/).

## What about expenses for a side gig?

Keep earnings and costs in separate categories. For example, use Photography income under Income and Photography supplies in an expense group. You can also [create a group](/help/articles/category-groups/) called Photography expenses and add categories for Equipment, Software, and Travel.

The [side-gig example](/help/articles/side-gig-income-and-expenses/) shows how those pieces fit together. For a work purchase someone will pay you back for, see [excluded categories and reimbursements](/help/articles/excluded-categories/).
