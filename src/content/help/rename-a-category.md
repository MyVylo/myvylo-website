Renaming changes the label and keeps the category’s existing transactions. For example, you can rename Dining out to Restaurants & takeout without moving any purchases.

The name alone doesn’t change how future purchases are sorted. Use [merchant choices](/help/articles/remember-merchant/) or [keywords](/help/articles/category-rules/) for that.
