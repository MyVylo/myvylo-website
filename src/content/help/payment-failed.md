Open **Settings → Billing** to see the payment status. If your card expired or was replaced, update it with the service managing the subscription.

For a web subscription, use [Vylo’s payment controls](/help/articles/payment-details/). For an App Store subscription, update your Apple Account’s payment method. Changing a card in web billing won’t change an Apple subscription.

## The bank declined the payment

Your card issuer may need to approve it or complete a verification step. Vylo can’t override that decision.

## I paid, but access still looks restricted

Reopen Billing and check that you’re in the right Vylo account and household. If Apple shows an active subscription, try [Restore Purchases](/help/articles/restore-subscription/).

If it still isn’t recognized, email **hi@myvylo.com** with the account email, billing provider, and approximate payment time. Don’t purchase a second plan to clear the message.
