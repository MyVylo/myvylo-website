Use the email address associated with your Vylo account. If you joined a household, it should be the address that received the invitation.

## Forgot your password?

Use the password-reset option on Vylo’s sign-in screen. Follow the newest reset email rather than creating another account.

## Why doesn’t an email link or code work?

It may have expired, already been used, or been replaced by a newer request. Request a fresh message, check spam, and use the newest link or code.

## Why was I signed out?

A session can expire or be revoked from another device. Sign in again and complete any requested verification.

If Vylo keeps returning to sign-in, email **hi@myvylo.com** with the device and error message. We won’t ask for your password or code.
